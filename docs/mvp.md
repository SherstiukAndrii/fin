# Fin MVP specification

Fin is a single-user, local, offline-first financial web application. The first version has **Bank** (holdings and payment records) and **Overview** (liquidity, net worth and projections). Windows/Linux browsers are supported through a locally running application.

The [domain model](domain-model.md) is normative for records, calculations and invariants. [Implementation details](open-questions.md) lists still-unresolved technical parameters. **All financial decisions Q01–Q16 have been approved**; they are not open questions. The [Investment Calculators architecture](investment-calculators.md) provides a modular path for OVGZ, shares/ETF, deposits and other assets, with future optional online data providers. The [future Statement Analysis architecture](statement-analysis.md) keeps bank-statement import and spending/opportunity analytics separate from the current MVP ledger.

## Bank — record positions and payments

- Start with a **dated closing snapshot**: cash and bank balances in native currencies, existing deposits and government-bond lots, and outstanding liabilities. Purchases made on or before that date are already reflected in the snapshot and must not debit cash a second time. Record subsequent purchases, receipts and adjustments as dated events.
- Enter deposits with bank, currency, principal, start/maturity dates, interest rate, contract terms and payment schedule. Enter government bonds with ISIN, currency, denomination and maturity; preserve purchase lots with purchase date, quantity, price, fees and their **complete coupon and principal schedules**. **One OVGZ holding can pay several coupons before maturity** (for example, three separate coupon dates), then repay principal at maturity. Each scheduled coupon is an independent cash-flow entry; do not collapse coupons into one maturity receipt, assume a universal payment frequency, or confuse coupons with principal. Support differing coupon amounts and dates and allow manual schedule adjustments. Some instruments may also have staged principal repayments, recorded distinctly.
- Record **expected payments separately from actual receipts** (scheduled date/amount versus one or more actual dates/amounts). Actual cash only changes on receipt; overdue and partially received amounts remain visible. Never recognize the same receipt twice.
- Record commissions and taxes per position or event; an entered zero is different from an unknown expense. Keep acquisition cost distinct from current market/estimated value. Store each price with its value, unit/currency, observation date and source.
- Show holdings, purchase cost, latest valuation with its date, scheduled versus actual cash flows, maturity and expected gain when all inputs are known. An optional `target_annual_yield` is comparison-only and must not rewrite schedules.

## Overview — Free, Fire and Total

- **Free** is the estimated amount available for use, with a cumulative liquidity-level selector:
  - **0 — available now:** unblocked cash and immediately accessible account balances.
  - **1 — super-liquid:** level 0 plus quickly convertible/marketable currency and comparable items not already counted.
  - **2 — highly liquid:** level 1 plus saleable stocks, ETFs, bonds and similar securities.
  - **3 — less liquid:** level 2 plus saleable real estate, vehicles and other property.
  Each item belongs to one additive level. At levels that require liquidation, use **net expected proceeds after fees, taxes, spread and other selling costs**, not the gross asset value. Flag unknown selling costs and show estimated access delay; level 3 is not instantly available.
- **Fire** is *hypothetical* yearly/monthly income if selected Free earns the portfolio's **historically realized net annual yield**. Recognize received interest, coupons and dividends plus realized trading gains/losses after fees and taxes; ignore unrealized price changes and principal repayments. Default yield window **12 months**, with selectable **3 / 6 / 12 months / all history**. Base the yield on net realized result divided by time-weighted invested **cost basis** (daily average, with annualization where the observation period is shorter than a year). Show the measured window, capital basis and a warning for short history. When reliable capital or return data is missing show **“no data”**, not an invented 0%; permit a separately labeled **manual scenario rate**. Fire is a diagnostic scenario, **never an additional asset or automatically booked income**.
- **Total / Net Worth** = current market/estimated value of **all assets minus outstanding principal and accrued obligations**. Show Assets and Liabilities alongside the net figure. Preserve purchase cost separately. If a valuation is old, display its observation date and warn; warning thresholds differ by asset type. Use the last known valuation with disclosure when a current value is unavailable.
- Display **Total Nominal** and **Total Real** simultaneously. UAH is the default reporting currency with a USD/EUR display switch; preserve all source amounts in native currencies. Real values use the **projection start date** as the inflation base (price index 1 on that date); a single manually entered effective annual inflation rate applies across the MVP horizon. Calculation design must allow year-specific rates later.

## Currency and data provenance

- Store each manually entered FX observation with pair/direction, value, date and source. For any historical valuation date, use the last rate observed **on or before** that date. Warn if it is **more than seven calendar days old** on the date valued. When a required rate does not exist, show native amounts and mark the converted aggregate **incomplete**, never silently use zero.
- In the future projection, **hold the last known FX rate constant as an explicit assumption**, with a prominent warning; do not present it as a forecast of exchange rates. This does not override historical conversion rules.
- The MVP is fully usable offline. Prices, FX, positions and forecasts are entered manually. Later, optional adapters may retrieve FX, securities prices, inflation and other information from official sources, without removing manual overrides or mutating the domain calculation rules.

## Forecast inputs and behavior

- Select a projection start date and a horizon up to **50 calendar years**. Use the dated initial snapshot and all later events; do not assume the start date is today or repeat past purchases.
- Enter **net monthly contributions** (not salary before expenses), with an effective-date schedule allowing the amount to change, and **one-off planned income/expense events**. Forecasts include planned events; actual cash remains based on confirmed events and monthly control snapshots. A reconciliation difference is an identifiable correction event, not a silent rewrite of history.
- Contractual payment schedules provide **every coupon/interest event at its own scheduled date**, as well as separately scheduled partial or final principal repayments. An OVGZ may pay several coupons during its life before final redemption. Track actual receipts independently and reconcile, rather than double-count, their planned equivalents as facts arrive. A coupon does **not** reduce the holding's principal or remaining purchase quantity. An instrument's redeemed principal stays in cash until the user explicitly schedules a new investment; **no automatic reinvestment**.
- Report projected cash, asset values, liabilities, Net Worth and inflation-adjusted Net Worth. Keep Fire and manually assumed scenarios visually separate from realized returns and **do not add Fire to projected Total**. Future valuation assumptions (including flat market prices and FX) must be labeled where used; no automatic market appreciation is implied.
- Present monthly forecast points over the full 50-year horizon, with daily detail for shorter periods and charts derived from the same dated calculations.

## Local storage, precision and data safety

- Use local SQLite; provide **regular local backups**, explicit manual export/restore and a tested restore/integrity path. Keep the service bound to localhost by default; remote access via the user's private VPN and authentication is a separate phase. Never put real balances or credentials in public Issues/PRs.
- Use `Decimal` for financial calculations and retain intermediate precision. Apply rounding when a real cash flow is posted or for currency/instrument-specific display; do not store all instruments as two-decimal `float` values. Distinguish zero, unknown, missing and invalid inputs.
- Automation of bank accounts, broker data, public pricing, cloud sync and historical CSV/XLSX import are **outside the first MVP**. The first dataset is entered as a manual snapshot.

## Planned extension — investment calculators and online data

- Reserve a **calculator service independent of Bank/Overview** for scenario comparison of OVGZ and other bonds, stocks/ETF, deposits and generic other investments. Manual bond/deposit calculations may be implemented in separate PRs as Bank features; advanced equity and other assets are staged extensions, not new mandatory features of the first MVP.
- **OVGZ:** calculate purchase outlay, dirty/clean pricing conventions, full dated coupon and principal schedule, gross/net proceeds, holding-to-maturity yield when valid, and sale-vs-hold scenarios; do not collapse multiple coupons or infer actual retail prices from auction results.
- **Stocks/ETF:** current valuation, weighted invested cost, realized/unrealized gains, net dividends, splits/corporate actions and scenarios without inventing guaranteed future dividends or price growth. Other assets reuse a generic dated cash-flow engine with type-specific rules.
- Provide optional *read-only*, provider-specific market-data adapters with caching, source/observation date, quote type, refresh status and manual override. Candidate data: official NBU OVGZ securities reference (ISIN/coupon dates), NBU FX, NBU auction results, licensed equity/ETF market prices; external calls must never block offline calculations. See [Investment Calculators architecture](investment-calculators.md).
- Calculator scenarios must not change real account balances or add Fire to Total; creating an actual holding requires a separate confirmed transaction.

## Planned extension — bank statements and spending intelligence

- Preserve the ability to import bank statements in a **future, standalone Statement Analysis module** to identify potentially unjustified expenses and missed financial opportunities. A user-initiated, read-only adapter will normalize per-transaction data (starting with CSV; other formats later), reconcile transfers, refunds, coupons and known events, and run explainable recurring-charge, duplicate-charge, fee, anomaly and opportunity rules.
- **Do not turn the MVP into transaction-by-transaction spending accounting.** Imported rows are a separate analytical source, not automatically ledger events. The dated opening snapshot and monthly reconciliations remain authoritative, and matched transactions must never double-count balances, investment yield or expenses.
- Findings must show evidence, estimated avoidable cost or hypothetical saving, assumptions, confidence and user dismiss/confirm feedback. Idle-cash versus deposit/OVGZ scenarios explicitly show liquidity needs, currency, taxes, fees and risk rather than promising returns. Never label an expense “unjustified” without an opportunity to review it.
- Keep processing **local and offline by default**, read-only, with no bank credentials or raw statements sent to cloud AI, logs, tests or public repositories. Future connectors/LLMs and any automated actions require separate user consent and design. See [Statement Analysis architecture](statement-analysis.md).

## Acceptance criteria (test with invented figures)

1. **Opening state and transfers.** With a closing snapshot of 1,000 UAH cash plus an already owned 200 UAH bond, recording its historical purchase does not debit the 1,000 again. A later purchase for 120 UAH moves value from cash into an investment and reduces Net Worth only by valuation/transaction costs, not the entire purchase price. Transfers among own accounts do not create external income.
2. **Scheduled versus actual.** A planned coupon of 20 UAH due on day 1 is not booked in actual cash that day. A receipt of 12 UAH on day 3 increases cash by 12 only; 8 remains overdue until actually received. Planned and actual versions are not summed twice.
3. **Liquidity and valuation.** A security with market value 100 UAH and known net sale proceeds 98 UAH contributes 100 to gross assets and 98 to the selected Free tier; it is not counted again as cash. Levels 0–3 are cumulative. Unknown selling tax/fee produces an explicit caveat rather than false precision.
4. **Total and currency.** Assets of 300 UAH and accrued liabilities of 50 UAH yield 250 UAH nominal Net Worth. Converting to USD/EUR changes display only. A missing required FX quote produces an **incomplete Total**, and a rate aged 8 days warns while one aged 7 days does not. Historical rates may not be replaced by observations from the future.
5. **Fire.** A net realized result of 12 UAH over 12 months and a daily-average invested cost of 100 UAH yields a 12% historical annual yield in a one-year example. A hypothetical 250 UAH Free then shows Fire of 30 UAH/year but **does not increase Total**. Exclude unrealized appreciation; allow 3/6/12/all-history views. Short histories are annualized with a warning; missing basis yields “no data”, not 0%.
6. **Monthly inputs and reconciliation.** A scheduled monthly net contribution can change at a specified effective date; a one-off expense affects projected cash on its date. A month-end observed balance that differs from projected balance produces a traceable correction, not fabricated historical income.
7. **Multiple OVGZ coupons and final redemption.** Example: two bonds, nominal 1,000 UAH each, pay 40 UAH **per bond** on each of three distinct dates (March 1, June 1, September 1). Forecast **80 UAH** on each coupon date (total **240 UAH**) and **2,000 UAH** principal at maturity on December 1, with no additional coupon unless separately scheduled. Each actual receipt affects actual cash only when received; any missed or partial coupon remains outstanding. Coupons do not release principal/cost basis; the final redemption releases the remaining principal exactly once. Do not automatically reinvest returned money.
8. **Inflation and horizon.** At the projection start, nominal and real Total are equal in the same currency. With zero inflation they stay equal at all projected points. Nonzero annual inflation uses compounding from the projection start. Show monthly data for 50 years and daily detail for short intervals; switching reporting currency does not rewrite source holdings.
9. **Offline and recovery.** Restart the app offline and confirm persisted positions, observations, events and valuations. Verify scheduled local backup, manual export/restore and record integrity using test fixtures. No real account details or API keys in tests.
