# Babki reference

**Non-normative.** This note records visual and workflow inspiration.

Babki informed the choice of workflows and the visual layout for Fin. The supplied exports show two views with different tasks.

- **Bank** puts instrument terms and individual purchases in separate tables. It keeps purchase cost, expected proceeds, and yield visible alongside the records and shows a summary across holdings.
- **Overview** groups cash, bank balances, and investment figures in a summary. It places forecast inputs near that summary and presents the daily projection as a date-ordered table. A combined line chart compares the projected series, and a second chart gives purchasing power its own view.

The [MVP specification](../mvp.md) and [domain model](../domain-model.md) define Fin's behavior.
